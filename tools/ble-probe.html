BLE Probe (read-only)

Lists what a Bluetooth light offers to a web browser: its name, its services and characteristics, and the Device Information strings. It never writes anything to the light. Results stay on this page; copy them yourself if you want to share them.

  • Close the light's phone app first (or turn off Bluetooth on the phone): most lights accept one connection at a time.
  • Use Chrome or Edge on a Mac or PC.
  • Choose mesh light… lists only devices that advertise Bluetooth Mesh (amaran / Sidus lights do), so the list is short. Choose any device… lists everything nearby.
  • Scan for mesh lights logs what nearby mesh devices advertise, without connecting. It needs chrome://flags/#enable-experimental-web-platform-features turned on. Only devices advertising Mesh are logged, so other nearby devices stay out of the results.
