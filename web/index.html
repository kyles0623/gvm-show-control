      About
      
    
      GVM Show Control  · 
      
    
      Unofficial controller, not affiliated with or endorsed by GVM. Runs in your browser: your lights,
      looks, cues and effects never leave it. The hosted app counts visits with GoatCounter (no cookies, no personal
      data; page, browser, screen size and country only); turn that off below. Works offline once installed.

      Pre-show check
      
  
      
    
      
    
        
    
        Each light that is on dims for a moment, one at a time, so you can see which is which, and has to
      confirm the change. Lights that are off or running an effect aren't flashed; they pass if they've answered in the last few seconds.
        
    
        
  
        
        



  
        Help
        
  
        
    
        Quick start
        
    
          
      
        1. Turn the light on and set it to App mode: press the small mode button on the light (it cycles through the modes) until the display shows APP. Close the GVM phone app.
          2. 
      
        2. Press Pair light (top left) and choose BT_LED.
          3. 
      
        3. Use the sliders, or tap a Look or Effect.
          4. 
      
        4. Blackout (or B) takes the light out; press again to restore. Set a fade time to fade instead of snapping.
          5. 
      
        5. For a show, open ☰ Cue list (C) on the Show tab: set the lights, press Record, repeat. During the show press GO (Space) for each cue. Pop out puts the list in its own window, e.g. on a second screen.
          6. 
      
        6. More lights: press + next to the light chips (or Settings → Lights → Add another light). Chips at the top choose which light the controls change (Tab cycles them); double-click a chip to rename a light, e.g. “Key”, “Fill”.
          7. 
      
        7. The Gel menu applies gel-style colors; the Effects tab lets you build your own effects from color steps.
          8. 
      
        8. Before the show press Check: every light must answer, and each one dims briefly so you can see which is which.
          9. 
      
        9. Press Lock before the show so nothing changes by accident — GO, Back and Blackout still work.
          10.

        Troubleshooting
        
    
        The light doesn’t appear in the list
          
    
          “Bluetooth is off or blocked”
            
    
            It connects, then drops — or won’t connect at all
              
      
            • The phone app is the usual cause: the light accepts one connection. Close the GVM app or turn the phone’s Bluetooth off.
              • 
      
            • Power-cycle the light, then press Connect.
              • 
      
            • Move closer; walls and bodies reduce range.
            
    
            The app asks me to pick the light every time
              
      
            • Remembering a light needs a recent Chrome or Edge. Update the browser.
              • 
      
            • If it still forgets, open chrome://flags/#enable-web-bluetooth-new-permissions-backend, enable it and relaunch.
              • 
      
            • Clearing browsing data for this site also clears the permission.
            
    
            Effects stutter or skip
              
      
            • Lower the effect’s Speed.
              • 
      
            • Keep the light closer to the computer.
            
    
            Changes don’t show up after an update
              
      
            • Reload with ⌘/Ctrl + Shift + R, or close and reopen the installed app.
              • 
      
            • The version in the lower-right corner tells you which version is running.
