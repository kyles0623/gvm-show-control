      About
      
    
      GVM Show Control  · 
      
    
      Unofficial controller, not affiliated with or endorsed by GVM. Runs entirely in your browser:
      nothing is sent anywhere except Bluetooth commands to your light. Works offline once installed.
      
  
      
      



  
      Help
      
  
      
    
      Quick start
      
    
        
      
      1. Turn the light on and close the GVM phone app.
        2. 
      
      2. Press Pair light (top left) and choose BT_LED.
        3. 
      
      3. Use the sliders, or tap a Look or Effect.
        4. 
      
      4. Blackout (or B) takes the light out; press again to restore. Set a fade time to fade instead of snapping.
        5. 
      
      5. While blacked out you can set up the next look behind the scenes: brightness, color and Looks change only the preview (marked NEXT) — the light stays dark until you press Restore, then comes up in that look. Starting an effect turns the light on.
        6. 
      
      6. Press Lock before the show so nothing changes by accident — Blackout still works.
        7.

      Troubleshooting
      
    
      The light doesn’t appear in the list
        
    
        “Bluetooth is off or blocked”
          
    
          It connects, then drops — or won’t connect at all
            
      
          • The phone app is the usual cause: the light accepts one connection. Close the GVM app or turn the phone’s Bluetooth off.
            • 
      
          • Power-cycle the light, then press Connect.
            • 
      
          • Move closer; walls and bodies reduce range.
          
    
          The app asks me to pick the light every time
            
      
          • Remembering a light needs a recent Chrome or Edge. Update the browser.
            • 
      
          • If it still forgets, open chrome://flags/#enable-web-bluetooth-new-permissions-backend, enable it and relaunch.
            • 
      
          • Clearing browsing data for this site also clears the permission.
          
    
          Effects stutter or skip
            
      
          • Lower the effect’s Speed.
            • 
      
          • Keep the light closer to the computer.
          
    
          Changes don’t show up after an update
            
      
          • Reload with ⌘/Ctrl + Shift + R, or close and reopen the installed app.
