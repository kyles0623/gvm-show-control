Lighting a small theatre for under $1,000

Five color LED lights on stands, the cables and tape to run them, and a laptop with the free GVM Show Control app as your lighting desk. This is the shopping list, what each item is for, and where each light goes. Total: about $900, not counting the laptop.

2. Why these lights

  • Both work with GVM Show Control today. The GVM 800D-RGB is fully verified. The Ace 25c has been tested on two real lights (pairing, brightness, white and color); some newer features are still being checked on it.
  • Both have full color. One rig can do a warm day, a blue night, a red alarm and a party look without changing gels.
  • Mixing brands helps. A laptop holds about 7 Bluetooth connections. Each GVM light uses one, but amaran lights share a single connection, so you can grow past 7 lights by adding amaran lights. (That shared connection is new and is still being confirmed on real lights.)
  • GVM for the main lights, amaran for the extras. The GVM 800D-RGB runs from mains power all night. The Ace 25c is small and battery-powered, so it can hide behind a bar or sit at the back of the house with no cable.

4. Power and batteries

  • GVM 800D-RGB: runs on its AC adapter. It also takes NP-F batteries (not included) if there's no outlet nearby.
  • amaran Ace 25c: built-in battery. amaran rates it about 70 minutes at full standard output and about 4 h 40 min in its low-power mode. For a full show, plug it into a USB-C PD charger, or keep it dim.
  • Charge every battery light the day before, and run the app's pre-show check an hour before doors.

6. Lights to wait on

The app also has untested support (marked β) for Neewer, Godox, Govee, Nanlite and Yongnuo lights and the cheap "ELK-BLEDOM" RGB strips. It's built from public documentation, and nobody has tried it on a real light yet (see the lights roadmap). If you already own one, try it and report the result from Settings → Lights. If you're buying for a show, buy the two lights above.

7. Questions

Is this bright enough for a stage?

For a small room (a black box, classroom, café or church hall, roughly up to 100 seats) with the front lights 3–5 m (10–16 ft) from the actors, yes. For a large proscenium house, no: you need conventional stage fixtures.

Do I need a lighting desk or DMX?

No. The laptop is the desk: looks, presets, fades and a GO-button cue list are all in the app, and it works offline.

Can I use the GVM or amaran phone apps too?

Use one controller at a time. Close the phone app before a show so it doesn't hold the light's Bluetooth connection.
